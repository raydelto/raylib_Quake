About, controls and credits

raylib Quake

id Software's 1996 Quake engine (the WinQuake software renderer, released under the GPL) running on raylib and WebAssembly, with the free shareware episode Dimension of the Doomed.

Controls

↑ ↓ ← →Move and turn (Quake's original keys; rebind them in Options → Customize controls)

TouchOn phones and tablets (or with ?touch=1; ?touch=0 turns it off): drag on the left half to move, on the right half to look; FIRE, JUMP and WPN (next weapon) on the right; MENU (Esc) at the top. In menus tap the picture for Enter, swipe or use the arrows to move, Y/N answer questions and ⌨ opens the phone's keyboard. Add &console=1 for a console button.

Settings (config.cfg) and saved games stay in this browser (IndexedDB). There is no multiplayer in the browser build: a page cannot open UDP sockets. Starting a new game from the menu while one is running does not ask for confirmation here. After quitting, reload the page to play again. Extra command-line options: ?args=-skill%202%20%2Bmap%20e1m2. The game draws at the window's size in device pixels, up to about 1920×1080 worth of pixels (vid_maxpixels; for example ?args=%2Bvid_maxpixels%20921600 for 1280×720 worth on a slow device). ?args=-width%20640%20-height%20480 keeps the classic 640×480, scaled to the window.

?renderer=gl plays with id's GLQuake renderer on WebGL 2 instead (smooth textures and lighting, drawn at the window's full size; vid_maxpixels and -width/-height do not apply to it). Settings and saves are shared with the software renderer. Software renderer · GL renderer
